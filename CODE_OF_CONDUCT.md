# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion focused on the work.
Unacceptable behaviour can be reported to **mail@datamatters.io**; reports are handled
confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies and is incorporated here by
reference.
